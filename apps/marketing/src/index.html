Isolation & privacy

A deliberately self-contained preview.

The demo uses bundled fictional data and runs entirely in the browser. It has no account, cookies, analytics, uploads, external assets, persistence, or background network activity. Any changes disappear when the page reloads.

Important: This is an educational product demonstration—not financial or investment advice, live trading, order placement or execution, an automated-trading system, a prediction service, or evidence of performance, returns, accuracy, or win rate.
